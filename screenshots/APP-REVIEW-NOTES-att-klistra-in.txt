DEMO ACCOUNT
Sign in with the email and password provided above. The account has Premium enabled and is pre-populated with links, themes, statistics and a claimed physical card, so every screen has content.

IMPORTANT: the app offers both Sign in with Apple and email/password sign-in. Please use the email and password above. Signing in with Apple would create a new, empty account.

PAYMENTS (3.1.1 / 3.1.3)
The app sells two different things. Premium is digital content and is sold EXCLUSIVELY through Apple In-App Purchase. There is no alternative purchase path and no link out to an external payment flow for digital content. Physical NFC cards are shipped goods and are paid by card via Stripe, which is permitted under 3.1.3(e). Restore Purchases is available on the paywall and under Account, Billing.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (1.2)
Public profiles contain user-generated content. Every public profile has visible Report and Block controls in the footer. Reports go to a moderation queue reviewed within 24 hours. Our terms of use contain a zero-tolerance clause for objectionable content, and violating accounts are suspended, which immediately removes the profile from public view. A standalone reporting page is linked in the app footer, and our contact address is published in-app.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
Settings, then Delete account. This permanently deletes the account and all associated data, releases any physical card for re-activation, expires the wallet pass and revokes the Sign in with Apple credential via Apple's token revocation endpoint.

NATIVE FUNCTIONALITY (4.2)
The app is not a repackaged website. It uses Sign in with Apple, native In-App Purchase via StoreKit, push notifications, Apple Wallet pass generation, and the camera for profile photos. It is also the companion app for our physical NFC cards: tapping a card opens the corresponding profile.

NFC
The physical cards are read by iOS itself. The app does not use Core NFC and therefore requests no NFC permission.
